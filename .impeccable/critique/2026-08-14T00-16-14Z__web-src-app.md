#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The app generally succeeds at the "instrument panel" aesthetic required by `DESIGN.md`. It relies heavily on flat design, single-pixel borders, and strong typographic hierarchy where numbers win out over labels. However, it still exhibits some generic "AI generated" tells.

**Deterministic scan**:
- ⚠️ **Side-tab accent border** detected in `globals.css` (line 1254). The thick orange left border on the Hot Lead cards is a classic AI-slop anti-pattern. `DESIGN.md` explicitly calls for removing these in favor of subtle background tints or top-bar accents.

#### Overall Impression
The web app is functionally solid and structurally clean. The core layout respects the House Flipping brand, using dark-on-light cards and `Terminal Blue` interactions. However, it suffers from a few sloppy execution details—most notably a broken popover, poor filter alignment, and an excessively dangerous global action button.

#### What's Working
- **Card Hierarchy**: The lead cards are excellent. The uppercase faint labels (`PRICE`, `€/M²`, `DISCOUNT`) successfully recede, allowing the bold black numbers to dominate the visual weight.
- **Cognitive Load**: Forms and settings follow Miller's Law closely, grouping items into clean sets of 4 or fewer.
- **Color Palette**: The UI sticks to the `Paper`/`Paper Elevated` backgrounds and restricts color usage effectively.

#### Priority Issues
1. **P0 (Critical)**: **Broken Popover Placement** on the Triage page. Clicking "Assign area" renders the popover at the absolute top-left of the window, overlapping the main navigation, instead of anchoring to the trigger button.
2. **P1 (High)**: **AI-Slop Side Borders**. The lead cards use a thick left-accent border. This needs to be replaced with a subtler design-system approved pattern (like a tag, subtle background tint, or top-border).
3. **P1 (High)**: **Filter Alignment**. The dashboard filters (Search, Discount, Area, Sort) are staggered vertically and unevenly, wasting space and looking unprofessional. They should be forced into a single row or a clean flex grid.

#### Minor Observations
- **Destructive Safety**: The "Wipe All Leads" button is bright red and positioned at the top right of the main dashboard. It's too prominent for a global destructive action and lacks an immediate safeguard (unless a hidden modal exists).
- **Alert Cards**: The "Resolve" and "Dismiss" buttons are stacked vertically, making the cards much taller than necessary. Aligning them horizontally would tighten up the list view.
